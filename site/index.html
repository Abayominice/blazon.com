Our product

Rosta: payroll your staff can trust

Rosta runs monthly payroll in 48 African countries — with Nigeria's 2026 PAYE signed off by a chartered accountant — tracks every tax and pension remittance with its receipt, and gives staff self-service payslips, leave and requests. Payroll bureaus and HR firms can run it under their own brand.

How Rosta works

What's included

  • Pay runs with two-person approval
  • Remittance deadlines, reminders and receipts
  • Salary payouts from your Paystack or Flutterwave balance
  • Leave, attendance, loans, expenses and documents
  • 30-day free trial, no card needed
