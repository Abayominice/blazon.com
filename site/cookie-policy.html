Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Blazonshots LTD uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on blazonshots.com, and what choices you have regarding them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website or a third-party service to recognise your device on future visits, remember preferences, measure website performance, or deliver relevant advertising. Cookies do not contain your name or personal contact details on their own.

Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function correctly. They enable basic features such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Analytics cookies: We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our website in aggregate — for example, which pages are visited most frequently, how long visitors stay, and where they came from. This data is used to improve the website and is not linked to individual identities.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense places advertising cookies on your device to deliver ads that are relevant to you based on your browsing activity on this site and other sites across the internet. These cookies are placed by Google and its advertising partners, not directly by Blazonshots LTD.

Google may use the DoubleClick cookie and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses your data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How to manage and opt out of cookies

You have several options for managing how cookies are used on your device:

Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions. Note that blocking all cookies may affect website functionality.

Google personalised advertising opt-out: To opt out of personalised advertising from Google, visit Google's Ads Settings. You can also use the Google Advertising Opt-out Plugin.

Industry opt-out tools: You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many participating companies through the Digital Advertising Alliance at www.aboutads.info/choices or the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies on our website are placed by third-party services such as Google. We do not control these cookies or how the data they collect is used. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties for more information. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made.
