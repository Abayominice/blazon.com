Apps & Products

Blazonshots LTD builds digital products with a focus on simplicity, performance, and genuine usefulness. Below are the products we have released and the platforms currently in development.

BlazonCalc

BlazonCalc is a clean, fast calculator application designed for everyday arithmetic. The app was built to be lightweight, load instantly, and work without unnecessary permissions or background data use. It does exactly what a calculator should do — reliably, with no distractions.

BlazonCalc was our first published app and reflects the design philosophy we apply across all our products: start with a single clear purpose, make it work well, and do not add features that serve the developer more than the user. The app is available on Android.

For support queries related to BlazonCalc, contact us at info@blazonshotsltd.com.

Blazonshots Platform — In Development

The Blazonshots Platform is our primary product currently in active development. It is being built as a student-focused digital platform for tertiary communities — a space where students can connect, discover relevant businesses and services near their campus, access useful tools, and engage with content built specifically for their environment.

The platform is being developed with particular attention to the real conditions most students face: varied device quality, inconsistent network speeds, privacy concerns, and the need for a trustworthy community environment. We are not building a generic social network. We are building something that understands the rhythms of campus life and adds genuine value to how students navigate it.

Key areas of focus for the Blazonshots Platform include student-to-student discovery and connection, local business visibility for campus-adjacent services, safety features including reporting tools and emergency contact support, a lightweight and fast interface that works across device types, and clear moderation with transparent community standards.

The platform is currently in internal development and testing. If you are a student, institution, or business interested in early access, partnership, or beta testing, we would be glad to hear from you.
