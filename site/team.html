Blazonshots Editorial Team

Who is behind the Blazonshots Blog, and how our articles are produced.

Abayomi Usman — Co-founder and editor

Abayomi Usman is a co-founder of Blazonshots LTD and sets the editorial direction of the Blazonshots Blog. He holds a BSc in Computer Science from Kwara State University and has worked across full-stack software development, technical product management and business development. From 2017 to 2023 he founded and ran ABTECHMAG LTD, a technical outsourcing agency. He is a Microsoft Certified Azure Administrator (AZ-104) and a Certified Product Manager.

At Blazonshots he leads product for Rosta, the company's HR and payroll platform, which is why the blog covers payroll and HR alongside student life and campus technology.

What we write about

Students and graduates: grades and CGPA, study skills, student money, SIWES and first jobs, and staying safe online. Employers and HR teams: running payroll in Nigeria and across Africa, PAYE, pension and other statutory remittances, leave and attendance, and practical guides to using Rosta. Builders: designing, launching and growing campus apps.

How articles are produced

Articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI writing tools, then checked against official and primary sources — such as the Nigeria Revenue Service, PenCom, NUC, JAMB, ITF or a university's own published rules — before publication. Where a rule or figure can change, we link to the source and date the article. Hypothetical examples are labelled as hypothetical; we do not invent statistics, quotes or case studies.

Payroll and tax articles explain how the rules work in general. They are not personal tax, legal or financial advice. Check your own situation with a qualified accountant or the relevant agency.

Our full standards are in the Editorial Policy.

Corrections

If you spot something wrong or out of date, email info@blazonshotsltd.com with the article link. We review every correction request and update the article where needed.
