Why Blazonshots built Rosta

Most of what we publish is for students and fresh graduates: how to raise a CGPA, write a SIWES report or read a first payslip. Rosta is the other side of the same journey. The students we write for become the interns, graduate trainees and staff of Nigerian businesses, and those businesses need to pay them correctly, deduct the right PAYE and pension, and remit to the right agencies on time. Many still do this in spreadsheets, which is where late remittances, wrong deductions and payroll fraud usually start.

Rosta is built for business owners and HR teams running payroll themselves, for companies switching from another payroll system, and for payroll bureaus and HR firms that run payroll for several clients under their own brand. Nigeria's 2026 PAYE calculation in Rosta has been signed off by a chartered accountant.

Payroll, step by step

Every month follows the same visible path: create the pay run, add one-off payments, calculate, submit for approval, then pay and send payslips. Each run moves through Draft, Calculated, Submitted, Approved, Paid and Closed, so everyone can see exactly where payroll stands.

  • Salary structures and pay elements: you decide how gross pay splits into basic, housing, transport and other allowances, and Rosta applies the right pension and NHF treatment.
  • PAYE your way: choose cumulative or annualised PAYE, pro-rate part months by calendar or working days, and approve employees' rent and other tax reliefs with proof.
  • Two-person approval: whoever prepares a pay run cannot approve it, and runs flag anyone whose net pay moves more than the percentage you set.
  • Payouts: pay salaries straight from your own Paystack or Flutterwave balance, with checks that each salary account belongs to the right person.
  • Switching mid-year: paste opening balances (taxable pay, PAYE, pension and NHF to date) from your previous payroll and carry on.
  • Gross-to-net calculator: work out net pay from gross, or the gross needed for a target net, before you make a job offer.

Remittances with proof

Rosta lists everything your approved pay runs owe to the State Internal Revenue Service, your employees' Pension Fund Administrators, the Federal Mortgage Bank of Nigeria (NHF), NSITF and ITF, with a due date for each and a place to record the payment and its receipt. People responsible for remittances are reminded before each deadline, the day after a deadline is missed, and again a week later.

Everything else HR does

  • People: employee records, spreadsheet import, departments, branches and grades, onboarding and leaver checklists, probation tracking, and confidential HR cases.
  • Leave and attendance: leave types, balances, holidays and approvals; clock-ins, schedules, lateness, overtime and timesheets.
  • Loans and benefits: salary advances and staff loans repaid automatically through payroll within Labour Act limits, plus health insurance and other benefit plans.
  • Expenses and requests: expense claims with receipts and limits, and custom request forms for letters, equipment or training.
  • Recruitment and performance: jobs, candidates, interviews and offers; review cycles, goals and one-to-ones.
  • Documents: policies staff must read and acknowledge, numbered letters on your letterhead, and reminders before documents expire.
  • Self-service: staff see their payslips, request leave, update their details and track requests without asking HR.
