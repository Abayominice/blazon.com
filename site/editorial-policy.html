Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Blazonshots Blog publishes practical guides for students and graduates, payroll and HR guides for employers in Nigeria and across Africa, and articles on building campus technology. Our goal is information that is accurate, specific and genuinely useful in real Nigerian conditions.

Who is responsible

The blog is run by the Blazonshots Editorial Team, led by co-founder Abayomi Usman, who sets its editorial direction and is responsible for what we publish.

How articles are produced

Articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI writing tools. Before publication, facts that can change — grading scales, tax rates, contribution rates, deadlines, prices and official procedures — are checked against official or primary sources such as government agencies, regulators, universities and product publishers, and we link to those sources. If a fact cannot be verified, we leave it out or say plainly that readers should confirm it.

Content standards

We do not invent statistics, studies, quotes, testimonials or personal stories. Hypothetical examples are labelled as hypothetical. We avoid clickbait and filler, and we write for readers, not for search engines.

Payroll, tax and HR content

Our payroll and tax articles explain how rules generally work. They are not personal tax, legal or financial advice. Rules change and individual situations differ, so check your position with a qualified accountant, lawyer or the relevant agency.

Our own products

Blazonshots LTD builds Rosta, an HR and payroll platform. Some articles explain how to do a task in Rosta, and payroll guides may mention it where relevant. We say so clearly, and we keep general guides useful whether or not you use our products.

Corrections

If information is outdated or inaccurate, we update, correct or remove it. Readers can request a review by emailing info@blazonshotsltd.com with the article link.

Advertising and independence

Advertising may appear on the website after approval from advertising partners. Ads do not determine our editorial topics or recommendations.
