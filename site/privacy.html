Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Blazonshots LTD ("we", "our", or "the company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have regarding your information when you use our website at blazonshots.com.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Information you provide directly: When you contact us via email or a contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message. We only use this to respond to your enquiry.

Information collected automatically: When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including your browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time of visit. This information is collected in aggregate and is used solely to understand how the website is used and to improve it.

Advertising-related information: We display advertising on this website served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to show you relevant advertisements. This may include the use of cookies and similar tracking technologies. Blazonshots LTD does not have direct access to the data collected by Google for advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiries and messages, to improve the content and performance of our website, to understand general visitor behaviour in aggregate, and to comply with our legal obligations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

This website uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for basic website functionality and for advertising purposes as described above. Please see our Cookie Policy for full details on how we use cookies and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact information (such as emails you send us) only for as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your enquiry, and no longer than required by applicable law. Website analytics data is retained in aggregate form only.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to information we hold about you, the right to request correction of inaccurate information, the right to request deletion of your information, and the right to object to or restrict certain types of processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, please contact us at: info@blazonshotsltd.com
