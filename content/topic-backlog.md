## Why these topics

The blog started with a narrow pillar (building campus apps). That audience is
small, so search growth is capped. These clusters keep the same identity —
practical, honest help for students, from a Lagos student-tech company — but
target questions Nigerian and African university students actually type into
Google every day. Each cluster has a pillar guide plus supporting posts that
link to each other, which is how a small site earns topical authority.

Rules for topic choice when the backlog runs low (the publisher may add new ones):
- A real question a student would search, phrased the way they would search it.
- Something we can answer better than the current top results: more specific,
  more current, more honest, worked examples.
- Evergreen or easily verifiable. No exam-result rumours, no "leaked" anything,
  no admission-guarantee or get-rich content, no medical, legal or investment advice.
- Fits one of the clusters below so it can link to and from existing posts.
