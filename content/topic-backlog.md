# Blazonshots blog — topic backlog

Each publishing day the scheduled publisher writes **two** articles:
1. the first unchecked topic in **Queue S (students)**, and
2. the first unchecked topic in **Queue R (payroll, HR and Rosta)**.

It ticks each one (`- [x] ... — published YYYY-MM-DD`) in the same commit.
Edit freely: reorder, delete or add topics. Whatever is at the top of a queue
goes out next.

## How the two queues fit together

Blazonshots builds technology for students and for the businesses that employ
them. The student queue answers questions students and fresh graduates search
for (grades, study, money, SIWES, first jobs). The payroll queue answers
questions employers search for (running payroll, PAYE, pension, remittances,
leave) and shows how Rosta handles them. Bridge topics (interns, SIWES
students, a founder's first hire) link the two.

Rules for topic choice when a queue runs low (the publisher may add 10 more):
- A real question someone would search, phrased the way they would search it.
- Something we can answer better than the current top results: more specific,
  more current, more honest, with worked examples.
- Evergreen or easily verifiable. No exam-result rumours, no "leaked"
  anything, no admission-guarantee or get-rich content, no medical, legal or
  investment advice.
- Queue R: at most every second topic may be a pure "how to do X in Rosta"
  guide; the others must be genuinely useful payroll/HR education that would
  help an employer even if they never use Rosta, with Rosta shown where it fits.
